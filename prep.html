48 hours before class

Get ready in about 10 minutes

Follow the bullets below. All you need is a free Muse account and your Google login — we connect everything live in class. (We’ll add a short welcome video here later.)

Simplest path (do this)

1

Create your free Muse account

Sign up for a free Muse account — no paid plan needed for class.

  • Everything we build in class works on the free plan.
  • Use an email you can reach during class — your confirmation and prep links go there.
2

Have your Google login handy

We’ll connect your Muse to Google Drive and contacts live in class. Just know your Google password (or have your phone nearby for 2-step verification).

3

Send Muse one message

Open your Muse account and send it any message — “hello” counts. You’re ready when it replies.

4

Pick one tool for class (don’t connect yet)

Choose one: Google Calendar, Gmail, or Google Drive. We’ll connect it live. Knowing which one is enough.

5

Class day basics

  • Laptop or desktop (phone is fine for watching; building needs a computer).
  • Quiet-ish spot + headphones if you have them.
  • Zoom ready (link comes in your class email).

Already have a paid Muse plan?
